// 03. ENGINEERING FAQ

Frequently Asked Technical Questions.

Everything you need to know regarding CAD submission, NDA protocols, quality certifications, and lead times.

What CAD model formats does Forgex accept for rapid toolpath quotation?

We natively accept neutral 3D CAD formats including STEP (.step, .stp AP203/AP214/AP242), IGES (.igs), and Parasolid (.x_t, .x_b). We also directly ingest native Siemens NX, CATIA V5/V6, PTC Creo, and SolidWorks files. For geometric dimensioning & tolerancing (GD&T), please attach PDF engineering drawings highlighting critical datums.

How does Forgex execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

We prioritize confidentiality. Submitting a request through our portal automatically initiates our bilateral mutual NDA under German and Delaware law. Alternatively, you can email your corporate standard NDA to legal@forgex-manufacturing.com for 4-hour legal review and digital counter-signature.

What is the expected turnaround time for First Article Inspection (FAI) prototypes?

For rapid prototype aerospace and automotive components, our Fast-Track workcells output verified First Article Inspection (AS9102 FAI) batches in 7 to 10 business days, complete with coordinate measuring machine (CMM) dimensional reports and full material test certificates.

What mill test certificates and metallurgical inspection data are supplied?

Every shipment is accompanied by EN 10204 3.1 inspection certification proving melt origin, chemical spectrometry, tensile yield strength, elongation, and Charpy impact values. Non-destructive testing (ultrasonic, eddy current, liquid penetrant) is performed in-house by Level II/III certified technicians.
