// 01. CIRCULAR PILLARS

Engineering a Closed-Loop Resource Cycle.

Every byproduct of our multi-axis CNC milling, cutting, and welding processes is captured, cleaned, and recycled back into high-purity production stock.

01 100% CLOSED-LOOP CHIP RECLAIM

Optical Alloy Sorting & Remelt

CNC swarf and metal turnings are automatically sorted by spectrometer and briquetted under vacuum to prevent oxidation. 100% remelted into certified billet stock without degradation.

SAVED: 4,800 TONS/YR VIRGIN ORE
02 12.8 MW RENEWABLE MICROGRID

Rooftop Solar & Thermal Storage

Our Munich and Detroit facilities feature vast rooftop bifacial solar arrays connected to 40 MWh liquid-cooled battery banks, supplying 82% of all annual machine spindle power directly.

CLEAN ENERGY: 16.5 GWH / YEAR
03 VACUUM CENTRIFUGE RECOVERY

99.4% Coolant & Oil Recirculation

High-speed centrifugal separators and vacuum distillation extract all cutting lubricants from chips. Bio-lubricants are filtered to 1 micron and pumped back into machine sumps.

ZERO HAZARDOUS WASTE DISPOSAL
04 CIRCULAR PACKAGING

Anodized Returnable Transit Vaults

Components are transported in reusable CNC-machined aluminum transit cases with RFID moisture and shock sensors, eliminating single-use wood crates and plastic bubble film.
